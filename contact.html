Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment to visit? ↓
No appointment is needed! Walk-ins are warmly welcomed every day between 10:30 AM and 9:00 PM. However, if you are bringing your bridal party or family for extensive wedding trials, booking ahead guarantees dedicated seating and trial room access.
Do you offer custom alterations for lehengas and cholis? ↓
Yes! We have expert in-house master tailors who adjust waistbands, ghera lengths, and blouse necklines so your chosen outfit fits you like a glove.
Where is the showroom located in Satara? ↓
Our showroom is located at 203, Bhavani Peth, Khan Ali, Sadashiv Peth, right off Rajpath in Satara (Pincode: 415002). It is easily reachable from all parts of Satara city.
Can I enquire about price and size over WhatsApp? ↓
Absolutely! Simply click on any outfit on this website or reach out directly at +91 94044 04408. Our team will send available sizes, close-up videos, and trial timings.
