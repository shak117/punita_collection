Our Legacy in Satara

Celebrating Tradition with Haute Couture Precision

Established in the bustling historical trading quarter of Satara at Bhavani Peth, Khan Ali (adjacent to Rajpath), Punita Collection (पुनिता कलेक्शन) has grown from a cherished regional store into the district's most renowned bridal and ethnic destination.

We believe an outfit for a milestone celebration is not simply fabric—it is a cherished memory in the making. Whether it is an elaborate multi-day wedding celebration, a grand sangeet night, or intimate Diwali and Ganesh festival pujas, we curate pieces that evoke grace, confidence, and cultural pride.

The Experience

Designed for Your Personal Styling Comfort

Stepping into Punita Collection is designed to feel celebratory and welcoming. Our spacious, two-level showroom features thoughtfully organized departments so every visitor finds their dream outfit without feeling rushed:

  • ✓ Ground Floor Festive Department: Hundreds of handcrafted cotton suits, everyday luxury kurtis, palazzo sets, and vibrant dupattas.
  • ✓ Mezzanine Bridal & Reception Suite: Private seating for brides and their families to try lavish velvet lehengas, reception gowns, and bridal cholis in complete privacy.
  • ✓ In-House Master Karigars: On-site alteration specialists who adjust blouse necklines, ghera lengths, and waistbands to guarantee perfection.
